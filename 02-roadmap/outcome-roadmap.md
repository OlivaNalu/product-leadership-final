Gate for moving beyond Now: Validate the 70% weekly workflow adoption target, 60% repeat usage target, and 70% perceived-usefulness target with pilot users. Also check record quality and real-world task completion. If adoption remains weak, revisit the workflow hypothesis rather than automatically adding features. NEXT — Remove the barriers to repeatable adoption Sequence these bets based on evidence from the initial pilot; they are not all simultaneous commitments. 1. Make field workflows dependable in poor-connectivity environments. Rationale: Prioritize if the pilot shows that unreliable connectivity prevents repeat usage or causes data loss. Otherwise, avoid building offline complexity before it is validated. 2. Ensure field teams see and act on the updates that matter. Rationale: Add push notifications for schedule changes when missed updates are shown to be a material cause of workflow failure or delayed action. 3. Turn field-generated information into timely, actionable project visibility. Rationale: Extend data models or dashboards only when validated field adoption creates a clear need for cross-project visibility and the existing architecture cannot support it adequately. 4. Preserve enterprise trust as field activity becomes the live system of record. Rationale: Address the broken Procore integration and compliance workflow gaps according to customer impact, contractual obligations, and data-integrity risk; these may need to move forward sooner if they threaten existing customers. 5. Reduce the effort required to meet enterprise compliance requirements. Rationale: Explore automated compliance checklists once the field data they depend on is sufficiently complete and reliable, or sooner where a contractual commitment demands it. LATER — Strategic bets, not commitments These items may be valuable, but they should not displace the field adoption experiment without a compelling new reason. Executive analytics and custom KPI dashboards Bet: Better field data could enable better executive decisions and strengthen enterprise retention. Revisit when customer evidence and data quality justify the investment. Real-time weather integration Bet: Contextual weather information could improve scheduling decisions. Revisit if project teams demonstrate meaningful operational value and willingness to prioritize it. AI-assisted RFI drafting Bet: Reduce the effort required to prepare requests for information. Revisit after validating demand, accuracy requirements, and the target user's workflow. Time-tracking module Bet: Expand Meridian's role in field operations and potentially support new commercial opportunities. Revisit based on contractual demand, strategic fit, and delivery cost. Subcontractor portal and document sharing Bet: Extend coordinated workflows beyond the primary contractor's employees. Revisit after the core field workflow is proven and external collaboration is a validated adoption barrier. Broad web UI refresh Bet: Improve perceived product quality and enterprise buying confidence. Defer a general refresh unless usability research or sales evidence demonstrates a material business problem. The strategic choices behind this roadmap - Now: Prove the core field workflow is worth choosing. - Next: Remove demonstrated barriers and convert field activity into dependable enterprise value. - Later: Pursue adjacent growth opportunities once the core adoption model is repeatable. One important caveat: the broken Procore integration and commitments to customers at risk of renewal should be assessed immediately, even if they are not part of the field adoption roadmap. Contractual obligations, data integrity, or imminent customer loss can override sequencing. The roadmap's central discipline is to avoid confusing a promising feature with a proven strategic bet. Each move forward should be justified by evidence that Meridian is becoming more indispensable to the field without weakening the enterprise product. NOW — Outcome-based strategic bets 1. We bet giving field teams a dedicated, intuitive mobile experience will make Meridian their preferred tool for capturing and tracking on-site issues, while providing project managers with timely, reliable project records.
