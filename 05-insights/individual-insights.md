# Individual Insights: Strategy Development Process

> Module 6 · Lead the Team That Builds with AI

## Friction points

> The hardest part was moving from a compelling product idea to a defensible business bet. It was easy to assume that a dedicated mobile app would increase adoption, but harder to prove that adoption would translate into better data, stronger retention, and financial returns. I worked through this by challenging assumptions, quantifying potential returns, and defining clear kill criteria.

## Key learnings

> I will keep pressure testing strategies before committing resources.  Including identifying the riskiest assumptions, quantifying the expected value, and setting measurable success criteria and explicit stop conditions. I will also keep distinguishing between delivering features and changing customer behavior in ways that create business value.

## "Aha!" moment

> The shift was realizing that field adoption is not the end goal but rather the engine of enterprise value. If foremen use Meridian consistently, the business gains more reliable, timely data for reporting and decision-making, which could strengthen customer retention. That connection made the strategy more compelling, but also made validating the link between adoption and retention essential.
